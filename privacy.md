# Prime Jr Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-29

Prime Jr ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our YouTube Creator Intelligence SaaS platform.

## 1. What Data We Collect

Prime Jr collects the following data through YouTube API Services:

- Your authorized YouTube channel data (videos, analytics, metadata)
- Public statistics (views, likes, comments, subscribers)
- Derived metrics (scores generated independently by Prime Jr)

We do NOT collect private analytics from channels you do not own. All data collection is strictly limited to channels you have explicitly authorized through OAuth.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To provide channel intelligence, optimization recommendations, and analysis
- We do NOT sell your data to third parties
- Derived metrics are clearly labeled as "generated independently by Prime Jr, not sourced directly from YouTube API"

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- Public statistics and derived metrics: stored up to 36 calendar months
- Other data (video titles, creator names, comments): refreshed or deleted within 30 days
- You can request deletion at any time

## 4. Your Rights

You may:
- Revoke Prime Jr's access to your YouTube data at any time via Google Account settings
- Request deletion of all your data by contacting us
- Request a copy of your stored data

## 5. YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using this service, you agree to be bound by:
- YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact us at privacy@primejr.com. We will process your request within 7 business days.

You can also revoke access via Google Security Settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
